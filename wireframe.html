The opportunity

Fans outside the gates are the largest untapped audience in sports.

Over 90% of fans follow the game from outside the arena, out of reach of the teams and leagues they love.

FanMore brings them into one rewards program, so fans earn all year, organizations reach a fanbase they've never seen, and partners get measurable ROI.

Proposed qualifying transaction

FanMore's next chapter.

Falconstar Ventures Inc. has entered into a binding letter of intent to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of FanMore Technologies Inc. as its proposed qualifying transaction. Under the planned arrangement, FanMore would become a wholly owned subsidiary of Falconstar and the resulting issuer would continue FanMore's business.

Completion is subject to a definitive agreement, due diligence, a concurrent financing, and TSX Venture Exchange, shareholder and other approvals. Trading in Falconstar's shares is halted pending the TSXV's requirements for resumption.

This section contains forward-looking information about a proposed transaction. There can be no assurance that the transaction will be completed as proposed or at all. The TSX Venture Exchange has in no way passed upon the merits of the proposed transaction.
