The path to becoming a public company

  1. Jul 27, 2026

    Falconstar completes its IPO and lists on the TSX Venture Exchange.

  2. Sep 22, 2026

    Binding letter of intent signed to acquire FanMore Technologies Inc.

  3. Oct 1, 2026

    Transaction to be completed by plan of arrangement.

  4. Next (expected)

    Completion of the transaction, with FanMore becoming a public company.

Completion is subject to a definitive agreement, due diligence, concurrent financing, and TSXV, shareholder and other approvals. Trading in FSTV.P is halted until then. The TSXV has not reviewed the merits of the proposed transaction.
