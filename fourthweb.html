Proposed qualifying transaction

Falconstar × FanMore

Falconstar Ventures Inc., a capital pool company listed on the TSX Venture Exchange as FSTV.P, has entered into a binding letter of intent to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of FanMore Technologies Inc. as its proposed Qualifying Transaction.

On completion, the resulting issuer would continue FanMore's business. Falconstar is led by Chief Executive Officer Giovanni Gasbarro.
